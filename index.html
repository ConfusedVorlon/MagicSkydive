Aeroporto di Palermo-Boccadifalco · Palermo, Sicily

We're bringing skydiving back to Boccadifalco, Palermo's historic city airfield, tucked beneath Monte Cuccio on the western edge of the city. It's been a civil airfield since the Air Force left in 2009, and it has hosted skydiving before, with jumps from 4,200 metres here in 2010. Now it's coming back full-time.

Climb out at altitude and the whole Conca d'Oro opens up below you: the great limestone bulk of Monte Pellegrino, the white sand and turquoise water of Mondello bay, the wild cliffs of Capo Gallo and the deep blue Tyrrhenian Sea running off to the horizon. Sicilian sun, mild winters and long, dry summers keep the skies open most of the year.

Your tandem skydive

Sixty seconds that rewire your brain.

No experience needed, just you and an expert instructor. The door opens, the wind roars, and the two of you leave the plane together. You get a minute of pure freefall, then the parachute opens and everything goes quiet. You float down over the Sicilian coast and land grinning.

How it works

Your tandem, start to finish

1 · Gear up

No experience and no training course needed. Your instructor gives you a short, friendly briefing, fits your harness and walks you through every step.

2 · Jump

Take a scenic climb along the coast to 4,000 metres, then step out together. You get a full minute of freefall before your instructor opens the parachute, and you can even take the steering lines on the way down.

3 · Relive it

Add a video and photo package to capture every second, from the door opening to the moment your feet touch the ground. You'll want proof.
